Space Works apps for iOS and Android

The Space Works mobile apps are open-source native apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, still in development, for makerspace members and staff that connect to a Space Works server for equipment, loans, bookings, events and check-in.

Space Works for iOS is a native SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad that targets iOS 17 and later. Space Works for Android is a native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose app with Material 3 that runs on Android 8 (API 26) and later. Both are in development with no App Store or Google Play release; Debug builds include a clearly labelled demo, and live payments, push and provider sign-in still need deployment configuration.

The Space Works hub has a Mobile apps module for attested device sessions, native push and the in-app payment sheet.

What you can do

Mobile apps, feature by feature.

For members

  • Inventory search, equipment requests, loans and loan renewal.
  • Space bookings, event registration and tickets.
  • A membership card and an inbox for updates.
  • Makerspace switching in the iOS app.

For staff

  • Permission-aware request review on iOS.
  • Issue, return and check-in flows with explicit review and evidence steps.
  • Scanning and evidence entry from the device.

Accounts and security

  • Password, phone, Google, Apple and organisation sign-in, MFA and account recovery on iOS.
  • Devices and sessions, sign out everywhere, data export and account deletion journeys.
  • App Attest and Keychain storage on iOS.

Native design and accessibility

  • iOS adapts to iPhone and iPad layouts with Dynamic Type, Bold Text and Reduce Motion support.
  • Android uses Material 3 with adaptive phone and tablet navigation and accessible text scaling.
  • Light and dark themes and Gilroy typography on both platforms.

Status and white-labelling

  • Both apps are in development, with no App Store or Google Play release yet.
  • Debug builds include a labelled demo to try member and staff flows without a backend.
  • Branding and an optional locked HTTPS server origin are set in branding/brand.json.

Install

Yours to run.

Free and open source under AGPL-3.0. Your data stays in your own database.

Builds from source; there are no store releases yet. Follow along on SpaceWorks-iOS and SpaceWorks-Android, and point the app at your own Space Works server.

Questions

Mobile apps, answered.

Are the Space Works apps on the App Store or Google Play?

+

Not yet. The Space Works iOS and Android apps are in development with no store releases; Android CI release builds are unsigned and Play distribution still needs owner signing.

Which devices do the Space Works mobile apps support?

+

The Space Works iOS app targets iOS 17 and later on iPhone and iPad. The Space Works Android app runs on Android 8 (API 26) and later, on phones and tablets.

What do the Space Works mobile apps connect to?

+

The Space Works mobile apps are clients of a Space Works server through a pinned OpenAPI contract. On iOS you choose a server on launch, or a deployment can lock one HTTPS origin.

Can I try the Space Works mobile apps without a server?

+

Yes, in Debug builds. Both Space Works apps include a clearly labelled, isolated demo of the member and staff flows; Release builds leave it out.

Can I rebrand the Space Works mobile apps?

+

Yes. Both Space Works apps read deployment branding and an optional locked HTTPS origin from branding/brand.json, and each has a white-label guide.
