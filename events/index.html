Space Works / Events

Event registration and ticketing platform

Event registration, tickets and check-in

Space Works Events is a free, open-source, self-hosted event platform for maker and community events that handles event pages, registrations, live QR tickets, door check-in, ID cards, member programmes, feedback and certificates.

Space Works Events lets you publish an event page, take registrations, send live QR tickets, check people in at the door, print ID cards, run a member programme and follow up with feedback and certificates, all from one self-hosted app you control. Events are free by default; when you charge, tickets are paid straight to your own Razorpay or Stripe account with no platform fee. There are no ads or third-party trackers, guests' data stays in your own database, and it is built on Yuyu by Abhiram N J.

What you can do

Events, feature by feature.

Plan and publish

  • Event pages in your colours with a cover photo editor, tracks, speakers, venues, sponsors, FAQs and resources.
  • Public, hidden-link, invite-only or approval-required events, with an option to list them on Discover.
  • Short links like /aB3x9Kq for every event.
  • Recurring series, organisation roles and event co-organisers.

Registration

  • Custom registration forms with ten question types, help text and show-only-if logic.
  • Capacity limits, waitlists and approvals, with undo.
  • Confirmation emails with a calendar invite, sent through a durable outbox.

Paid tickets (optional)

  • Buyers pay into your own Razorpay or Stripe account, with no platform fee.
  • Fixed or pay-what-you-want prices, group tickets, extras, promo and access codes, members-only tickets and series passes.
  • Refunds, transfers, pay-at-the-desk, PDF receipts, chargeback alerts and an orders CSV.

Tickets and the door

  • Live QR tickets that tear off their stub on the guest's phone at check-in.
  • A check-in desk for QR, search and manual check-in, with undo.
  • Offline check-in that keeps scanning when venue Wi-Fi drops and syncs later.
  • PIN-locked door stations so volunteers can check people in without an account.

Badges and member IDs

  • Colour ID cards and badges with your logo, the guest's check-in QR and chosen registration answers.
  • A badge designer with stock sizes such as A6, 4 x 6 in and CR80, and one print-ready PDF.
  • Member ID programmes with a join form, CSV or Google Sheet import and approvals.
  • A verify QR on every member card that shows Valid, Expired or Revoked.

Follow up and run it safely

  • Broadcasts with 13 starter templates, five email designs, a review step and a five-second undo.
  • Feedback forms, a registration funnel in analytics and certificates built on your own artwork.
  • Required authenticator-app MFA for organisation staff and a breached-password check.
  • No ads and no third-party trackers.

Install

Yours to run.

Free and open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Your data stays in your own database.

On a Linux server
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SpaceWorks-Labs/SpaceWorks-Events/main/install.sh | bash

Run it as a user with sudo. Read the script first: install.sh.

Prefer the cloud? Deploy to Render in one click.

Questions

Events, answered.

Is Space Works Events free?

+

Yes. Space Works Events is free, open-source software under the GNU AGPL v3.0 or later, and events are free by default. It has no ads and no third-party trackers.

Does Space Works Events take a cut of ticket sales?

+

No. When you charge for an event, buyers pay on Razorpay's or Stripe's page and the money goes straight to your own account, with no platform fee.

Can Space Works Events check people in without internet?

+

Yes. Space Works Events can download the roster for offline check-in, keep scanning when the venue Wi-Fi drops and sync when you are back online. PIN-locked door stations let volunteers check people in without an account.

How do I install Space Works Events?

+

Run the one-line curl installer on a 64-bit Linux server; a small instance needs 2 vCPU, 2 GB RAM and 30 GB SSD. An optional cloud setup runs on free plans with Render, Supabase, Upstash, Cloudflare R2 and Brevo.

Can Space Works Events connect to other systems?

+

Yes. Space Works Events has a tenant-bound machine API (v1) with scoped, rotating credentials for connecting other systems.
