07 / The foundations are covered

Made to run.
Built to stay yours.

These come with every install, whichever areas you turn on.

Encrypted, signed backups

Full-platform backups where each space is sealed to its own keys, the whole archive is signed, and restore checks the signature before it decrypts anything. Offsite copies are verified and re-read every 30 days.

Safe updates

Updates come from GitHub releases. A backup is taken first, and the old version comes back if the new one fails its health check.

Move a space between servers

Export one makerspace with its records, photos and money, and import it on another server.

Private by design

Personal data is encrypted per space, integration secrets are encrypted at rest, and the audit log, evidence photos and QR scans can’t be edited.

Events, fully run

Series, approvals and waitlists, QR check-in with an offline roster and PIN stations, feedback, attendance certificates, badges and calendar feeds.

3D printing

Public print requests, printers and spools, filament tracking, slicer estimates and an optional charge at collection.

Live console, one search box

Queues, loans and check-ins update the moment they change, and every list has search that forgives typos.

Sign in your way

Password, Google, Apple, phone and SMS code, or your own identity provider: Keycloak, Authentik, Azure AD, Okta.

Roles you define

Editable roles per space over a fixed set of actions, four ready-made defaults, and a separate control plane for the server owner.

Maker profiles

Opt-in profiles with projects and interests, and a member directory that lists only the people who chose to be listed.

Organizations and many spaces

Run many makerspaces on one server, each fully separate, and link them under a network, university or chain with one event catalogue.

Runs small, runs watched

A single-box mode for one small server, request IDs through every log, and a Prometheus endpoint for queues, deliveries and storage.

08 / Good questions

Questions,
answered.

Still unsure? Ask the community or read the docs.

What is Space Works?

+

Space Works is free, open-source, self-hosted software for makerspaces, fab labs, hackerspaces, tool libraries and the communities around them. The main platform covers inventory and tool lending, machines and 3D printing, events, bookings and memberships in one install. Six standalone apps cover one of those jobs each.

Is Space Works free?

+

Yes. Every Space Works project is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0). There is no licence fee. You can use, study, modify and share it, including for commercial work, and you pay only for the server you host it on.

How do I install Space Works?

+

On a Linux server, as a user with sudo, run the one-line installer: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SpaceWorks-Labs/SpaceWorks/main/install.sh | bash. It asks every question first, changes nothing until you confirm, and runs the platform with Docker Compose. The documentation covers manual installs and hosting options.

Do I need the whole platform or one app?

+

Start with the main Space Works platform if you run a whole makerspace. If you only need one job done, the standalone apps (Inventory, Machines, Events, Bookings, Organization and Workspace) each install on their own with their own database. Running several apps does not automatically combine their accounts or data; they connect through REST APIs and webhooks where supported.

Can one installation run several makerspaces?

+

Yes. One Space Works server can host many makerspaces, each fully separate with its own members, inventory, roles, settings and audit trail. Networks, universities and chains can link spaces under one organisation with a shared event catalogue.

Does Space Works take payments?

+

Payments are optional. The main platform supports Stripe or Razorpay checkout; without them, fees and deposits are tracked and settled at the desk. The standalone Events, Bookings, Machines and Organization apps take Razorpay, Stripe or offline payments, and Organization adds UPI and GST receipts for Indian organisations.

Is there a mobile app?

+

Native iOS (SwiftUI, iOS 17 and later) and Android (Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Android 8 and later) apps are in development. Both connect to your own Space Works server for equipment, requests, loans, bookings, events and QR workflows.

Where is my data stored?

+

On your own server. Space Works is self-hosted, so member, inventory and payment records stay in your database. Personal data is encrypted per space, integration secrets are encrypted at rest, and backups are encrypted and signed.

Who makes Space Works?

+

Space Works was started by Shaan Shoukath at TinkerSpace Kochi, a community makerspace by TinkerHub in Kerala, India, and is built in the open by the Space Works community on GitHub. It runs at TinkerHub Kochi and TinkerHub Calicut, serving more than 1,000 members.
